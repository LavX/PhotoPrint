### Modifications

Done once, at import time, from the original 3640 x 2730 download:

- Auto-orient (no rotation was needed; the original is landscape), then fit to
  1200 x 900 with a centre crop. The original is already 4:3
  (3640 / 2730 = 1.3333), so the centre crop discarded no content.
- Downscaled to 1200 x 900 px (4:3).
- Baseline JPEG, quality 90, 4:2:0 chroma subsampling.
- All EXIF, XMP, IPTC and ICC metadata stripped. No other retouching, no colour
  or tone adjustment.

Result: 1200 x 900 px, 242 KB, sRGB, SHA-256
`b126d75482dc27db24238282337a9d564284a0bf4bbf7b6036d109d6c3302a06`.

### Why this image

A dusk harbour: cool blue sky and teal water against warm pink/orange cloud and
warm wooden jetty planks. It has proper highlights (bright cloud tops, water
speculars, white boat cabins), deep shadows (jetty posts, boats), a large neutral
grey-brown surface in the decking for judging white balance, and saturated
colours in three families (blue, magenta, warm yellow). That spread makes
saturation, split-tone, fade, grain and monochrome recipes distinguishable in a
160 px thumbnail. No faces, no logos, no legible text at preview size.

Bundled file: `resources/samples/effect-sample.jpg`.
